Infrastructure assessment questionnaire

Materials data infrastructures · standards · access · interoperability

Important: You must submit your completed questionnaire manually. Your answers are saved only in this browser; they are not submitted automatically.
  1. Complete the five sections for each infrastructure you want to report, and optionally suggest community resources in the box before downloading.
  2. At the end, click Download all infrastructures (JSON + PDF ZIP) and save the ZIP file to your computer.
  3. Open the Materials Commons SharePoint – Questionare Answers folder and upload the downloaded ZIP file there. The ZIP contains the JSON and PDF responses for every infrastructure you entered, plus separate JSON and PDF files for community resources (if provided).
Your assessment is submitted only after the ZIP has been uploaded to SharePoint. SharePoint access is required.
